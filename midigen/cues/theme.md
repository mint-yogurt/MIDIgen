---
name: Theme
aliases: opening, opening titles, theme song, intro theme, title music
---
Opening titles or a show's theme. Its job is identity: the audience should recognise it next week.
- Get to the hook fast: the main motif within the first two bars, or after a very short pickup or drum fill.
- State the motif, repeat it, then vary or answer it once. One strong idea beats three weak ones.
- Full arrangement: this is the one cue where the music is the star, so all the colours can be used.
- End with a clear button (a final hit or a short held tonic chord). Editors cut to the first scene on it.
