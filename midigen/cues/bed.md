---
name: Bed
aliases: underscore, background, under dialogue, voiceover bed, music bed, background music
---
A bed that sits underneath dialogue or a voiceover. The voice is always louder than the music, and the
music must never compete with it.
- Keep the middle register clear, where the voice lives (roughly G2-C6): no lead melody there, no busy
  comping. Use pads, a low bass, soft high sparkle (C6 and up) and light, steady rhythm.
- Keep it steady. No big builds, no sudden hits or breaks, no dramatic key changes: anything that grabs the
  ear pulls it away from the words.
- Phrases in even lengths (4 or 8 bars) with no strong cadence in the middle, so an editor can cut or loop
  it at any bar line.
- Track volumes on the low side (around 60-90). The ending can be simple (it will often be faded or cut).
