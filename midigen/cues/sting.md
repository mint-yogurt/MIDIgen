---
name: Sting
aliases: bumper, act break, transition, ident, stinger, button
---
A short sting: a bumper into or out of an ad break, an act break or a transition. Usually 2 to 6 seconds.
- One musical gesture only (a rising run into a hit, a three-chord flourish, a motif plus a chord). No intro.
- Every part lands together at the end: a crisp button, or a held chord that rings out.
- If a theme exists, a sting is usually its first few notes.
- Make it instantly readable as "something is ending" or "we're back".
