name: Jingle
aliases: commercial, ad, advert, advertisement, commercial jingle
---
Music for a TV or radio commercial.
- Open with a short intro (a drum fill, a pickup or a stab) and set the energy immediately.
- Write the hook so it could carry a sung tagline: mostly stepwise, inside about an octave, rhythmically
  simple, and repeated. The product name would land on its longest note.
- Ads usually run a voiceover over the middle, so after the opening statement the middle can thin out
  (fewer parts, no busy lead line) before the hook returns for the payoff.
- End with a big, unmistakable final hit on the tonic: the moment the logo appears.
