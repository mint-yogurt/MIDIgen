---
name: Credits
aliases: end credits, outro, closing, closing theme, sign-off
---
Closing credits or an outro.
- If there's a theme, reprise its motif, perhaps slower, thinner or in a new instrument: a fond goodbye.
- It can breathe more than a theme: a longer phrase, a gentler arrangement.
- End definitively: a resolved final chord that rings out, or a last statement of the motif and a button.
