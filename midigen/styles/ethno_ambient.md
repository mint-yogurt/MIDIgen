---
name: Ethno-ambient / tribal ambient
aliases: ethno-ambient, ethno ambient, tribal ambient, ethno-ambient/tribal ambient, tribal
tempo: 60-100
---
Late-80s and 90s tribal ambient (Steve Roach, Robert Rich, Vir Unis): hypnotic hand drums and drones in a
vast, dusty space. Ritual, earthy, timeless.
- Rhythm: interlocking hand-percussion ostinatos (congas, bongos, taiko, toms, shakers, woodblocks,
  claves) building a trance, not a backbeat. No kit backbeat.
- Harmony: drones and one or two chords at most; minor, Dorian or Phrygian; slow modal melodies.
- Sounds: low synth or bowed drones (a didgeridoo stand-in), dark atmospheric pads, breathy flutes (pan
  flute, shakuhachi, ocarina), kalimba, voices in the distance. Pitch bends make flutes and drones feel
  alive.
- Evolution: add and remove layers slowly; let the groove hypnotise.
- Avoid: Western pop chord progressions, bright major keys, tidy cadences.
