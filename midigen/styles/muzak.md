---
name: Muzak
aliases: elevator music, lift music, easy listening, beautiful music
tempo: 80-110
---
Elevator and background music in the 1960s-80s "beautiful music" tradition: lush, polite, unobtrusive,
designed to be heard, not listened to.
- Harmony: diatonic with maj7 and 6th chords, secondary dominants, gentle chromatic passing chords;
  everything resolves and nothing surprises.
- Sounds: a big soft string section, a lead on flute, clarinet, oboe, vibraphone or soft piano, an
  electric piano or harp comping, upright or soft electric bass in two-feel, brushed drums or a very light
  kit.
- Dynamics flat and mid-range: no accents, no climaxes. Melodies sound like a pop tune's chorus played
  very politely.
- Avoid: dissonance, heavy drums, loud bass, solos, anything that demands attention.
