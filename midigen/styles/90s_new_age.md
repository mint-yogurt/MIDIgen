---
name: 90s new age
aliases: new age, 90s newage, newage
tempo: 60-90
---
1990s new age, the Windham Hill and Narada catalogue, Enya, Vangelis, Kitaro and Yanni end of things:
spacious, serene, sincere, spiritual without being religious.
- Harmony: diatonic, often modal (Dorian, Aeolian, Mixolydian); sus2 and sus4, add9; drones and pedal
  tones; slow harmonic rhythm; Celtic-tinged melodies sit well.
- Sounds: warm pads, solo piano or harp, flute or pan flute, choir, strings, bells. Drums usually absent:
  at most soft hand percussion or a gentle frame-drum pulse.
- Melodies: long-breathed, singable, often doubled an octave apart; generous space between phrases.
- Production: long, lush reverb and slow attacks; dynamics swell rather than hit.
- Avoid: busy rhythms, backbeats, jazz chromaticism, sudden endings.
