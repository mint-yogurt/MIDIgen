---
name: Atmospheric drum and bass
aliases: atmospheric dnb, atmospheric drum & bass, intelligent dnb, intelligent drum and bass, liquid dnb
tempo: 160-178
---
Mid-1990s atmospheric ("intelligent") drum and bass: fast, busy breakbeats under slow, lush, jazzy
harmony. The tension between racing drums and floating chords is the whole point.
- Tempo about 165-175. Drums: two-step breakbeat (kick on 1 and the "and" of 3, snare on 2 and 4) with
  ghost snares (velocity 30-55) and 16th-note hats or ride. Vary the break every 2-4 bars. An acoustic or
  room kit sounds more like a sampled break than an electronic one.
- Bass: deep sub-bass in long notes that move at half the speed of the drums (a half-time feel).
- Harmony: minor 9ths, major 7ths, 11ths; two- or four-chord loops that drift rather than resolve.
- Sounds: lush pads, Rhodes-like electric piano, strings, choir "aahs", flute, bells. Heavy reverb on
  everything except the drums and bass.
- Avoid: four-on-the-floor kicks, tempos under 160, aggressive "jump-up" stabs, busy melodies.
