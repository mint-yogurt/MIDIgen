---
name: Corporate smooth rock
aliases: corporate rock, smooth rock, soft rock, aor, corporate video rock
tempo: 100-125
---
Polished late-80s and 90s soft rock / AOR as heard in corporate and training videos, industrial films
and sales conferences: motivational, confident, slick and slightly cheesy. "Teamwork. Excellence. You."
- Drums: a straight rock kit with a big gated-reverb snare on 2 and 4, eighth-note hats, a tom fill into
  each section, a crash on the downbeats.
- Sounds: a clean or chorused electric guitar lead (or overdriven for the big moment), synth pads, DX-style
  electric piano or piano, picked or fingered bass in driving eighths, synth-brass stabs.
- Harmony: major, I-V-vi-IV and IV-V-I; a suspended chord before the chorus; a key change up a step for
  the finale.
- Shape: a short intro, a confident hook, a lift, a strong final hit.
- Avoid: grit, dark minor keys, anything genuinely rebellious.
