---
name: Corporate new age
aliases: corporate newage, corporate ambient
tempo: 80-110
---
The polished, optimistic music of late-80s and 90s corporate videos, hotel lobbies, airline safety
videos and local forecasts: new-age calm made confident and on-brand. "Our solutions, your future."
- Harmony: bright major keys, add9 and sus chords, IV/I and V/I over a tonic pedal, uplifting moves to IV;
  a key change up a step for the big moment.
- Sounds: DX-style electric piano, warm pads, synth strings, fretless or fingered bass, soft saxophone,
  flute or piano melody, bells for sparkle; a light drum machine or soft kit with shakers.
- Feel: steady, gently propulsive, never edgy. The melody is clean and aspirational.
- Avoid: darkness, dissonance, heavy drums, anything that sounds like a risk.
