---
name: Mallsoft
aliases: mall soft, mall music, vaporwave mall
tempo: 60-90
---
Mallsoft (a 2010s vaporwave offshoot): late-80s and early-90s shopping-mall music heard echoing through
an empty food court. Nostalgic, dreamy, a little melancholy; consumerist paradise as a half-remembered
memory.
- Source idiom: smooth jazz, muzak and easy listening, but slowed down and far away.
- Harmony: lush major 7ths and minor 9ths; ii-V loops; short 2- or 4-bar loops repeated with little
  development (the repetition is the aesthetic).
- Sounds: electric piano, vibraphone, soft saxophone or flute, fretless bass, synth strings, a polite drum
  machine or brushed kit. Velocities soft, tones mellow.
- Distance: lots of reverb (hall or cathedral, high reverb sends), parts slightly behind the beat, nothing
  bright or punchy.
- Avoid: drama, big builds, crisp attacks, a clear ending (fading out mid-loop is very on-genre).
