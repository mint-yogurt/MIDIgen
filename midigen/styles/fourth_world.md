---
name: Fourth World
aliases: fourth world music, possible musics
tempo: 70-100
---
Jon Hassell's "Fourth World" idea (from 1980, with Brian Eno): an imagined unified music that is
primitive and futuristic at once. Hazy, sensual, nocturnal, mysterious.
- Lead: a treated, breathy horn line (trumpet or muted trumpet) that slides with pitch bends and
  microtonal inflections, drenched in reverb and chorus. Phrases are vocal and improvised-sounding.
- Rhythm: slow, sinuous polyrhythmic grooves on hand percussion (congas, bongos, talking-drum-like toms,
  shakers), sometimes with a hypnotic bass ostinato.
- Harmony: static, one or two chords or a drone; modal, unresolved.
- Texture: ambient synth beds, kalimba or marimba figures, soft treated sounds; lots of space.
- Avoid: Western functional cadences, straight backbeats, clean "pretty" melody.
