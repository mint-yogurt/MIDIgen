---
name: Ambient
aliases: ambient music, eno ambient
tempo: 50-80
---
Ambient in the Brian Eno and Harold Budd sense: music "as ignorable as it is interesting". Still, spacious,
slowly changing.
- Pulse: weak or absent. Long notes, slow attacks, silence as an instrument.
- Technique: overlapping figures of different lengths (for example a 5-beat phrase against a 7-beat
  phrase), so the combination keeps shifting.
- Harmony: static or very slow; pentatonic or diatonic clusters, open fifths, soft maj7 or add9 colours.
- Sounds: soft pads, a sparse piano with long reverb, bells or mallets, drones, airy textures.
- Dynamics low and even; let the reverb fill the room.
- Avoid: drums, busy melody, builds and climaxes, strong cadences.
