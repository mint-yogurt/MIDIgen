---
name: Utopian scholastic
aliases: utopian, scholastic, educational, edu-tv
tempo: 70-100
---
Background music for educational TV from an idealised future, made around 1985-1993: science
programmes, planetarium shows, museum kiosks, library documentaries. Calm, curious, optimistic, warm and
slightly nostalgic: a sense of wonder, never drama.
- Harmony: major keys with added colour (maj7, add9, sus2), Lydian touches (the raised 4th, or a II chord
  over a tonic pedal) for wonder, slow chord changes (one per bar or two).
- Sounds: warm synth pads, mallets and bells (celesta, vibraphone, glockenspiel, music box), a soft lead
  (calliope, flute, sine), piano or electric piano, gentle arpeggios. Drums sparse or none: perhaps a soft
  shaker or a light electronic pulse.
- Movement: slowly unfolding arpeggios, a melody that asks a question and then answers it.
- Avoid: trailer-style drama, minor-key dread, big drums, fast tempos.
