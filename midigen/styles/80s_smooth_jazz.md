---
name: 80s smooth jazz
aliases: smooth jazz, quiet storm, contemporary jazz, weather channel jazz
tempo: 80-105
---
1980s smooth or contemporary jazz (Spyro Gyra, Grover Washington Jr., Bob James, David Sanborn, early
Kenny G) and local-forecast music: slick, warm, sophisticated, laid back.
- Lead: soprano or alto saxophone (or flute or synth lead) with expressive bends, grace notes and
  sustained notes; an answering electric piano.
- Rhythm: tight light funk or a Latin-tinged groove, with rimshots or a soft snare on 2 and 4 and 16th
  hats or ride. Fretless or slap bass, syncopated.
- Harmony: maj7, min9, 11th and 13th chords; ii-V-I; slash chords (D/E, G/A); chromatic passing chords; a
  key change for the last chorus.
- Sounds: DX-style electric piano, synth pads or strings, light percussion (shaker, congas).
- Avoid: dissonance, swing-era big-band feel, distorted guitars.
