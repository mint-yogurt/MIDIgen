---
name: Exotica
aliases: tiki, lounge exotica
tempo: 90-130
---
Late-1950s and 60s exotica (Martin Denny, Les Baxter, Arthur Lyman): an imaginary, cocktail-lounge
version of tropical islands and faraway jungles. Lush, playful, mysterious, kitsch.
- Rhythm: Latin feels (bolero, rumba, beguine, mambo) on bongos, congas, claves and maracas; upright bass.
- Sounds: vibraphone and marimba up front, piano, flute, strings, celesta or chimes, a gong to open or
  close, bird calls and animal cries.
- Harmony: lush jazz chords (maj7, min7, 9ths), chromatic movement, minor-key melodies with "exotic"
  scales (harmonic minor, pentatonic).
- Drama: a mysterious intro, a lilting groove, a grand ending (gong, a vibraphone tremolo, a held chord).
- Avoid: modern drum-machine beats, synth leads, rock drums.
